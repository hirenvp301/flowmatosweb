Three-step workflow
How ReachMint works
01

Campaign Setup + AI Discovery

You describe your product, target audience, and outreach goal (e.g. "50 beta signups", "10 partnership conversations"). Claude then discovers 8 real, active communities across Reddit, HackerNews, LinkedIn, IndieHackers, Slack groups, Discord servers, and forums — each with a platform-matched tone and 5 ready-to-use search queries. Discovery takes under 30 seconds.

Claude (Anthropic) · Multi-platform · Tone-aware discovery
02

Script Generator

Paste any thread URL or raw text into the Script Generator. Claude identifies the pain point, reads the community tone, and writes a reply script that leads with genuine insight — not a product pitch. Tone is automatically matched to the community: technical for HN, startup-friendly for IndieHackers, professional for LinkedIn. The script always positions your product as a natural next step, never a hard sell.

Claude · Tone matching · Insight-first scripts · No spam
03

Goal Tracker

Every thread you reply to gets logged with the community, platform, status (drafted, posted, converted), and a link. The tracker shows your live progress toward the campaign goal — how many interactions you've made, how many converted, and what's still in flight. Multiple campaigns stay fully isolated: different goals, communities, and trackers per campaign.

Goal progress · Status tracking · Multi-campaign · Supabase
