How to Test SaaS Onboarding for Friction with AI

Hiren Patel · June 2026· 9 min read

The team that built your onboarding cannot see its friction — they know where every button is and why every step exists. Your new users do not. OnboardLens closes that gap: it sends Claude through your real product and website as your ICP persona, narrates exactly where it gets stuck, scores the friction, and writes the prioritized action plan to fix it.

The friction you cannot see

Every team is too close to its own product to experience it like a first-time user. You know that the third setup step is optional, that the integration list can be skipped, that “Configure workspace” means “invite your team.” A new user knows none of that. The exact moments that make them hesitate, backtrack, or quietly close the tab are invisible to the people who built the flow.

Analytics confirms the damage but never explains it. Mixpanel and Amplitude tell you that 40% of users drop at step three — not why. So teams guess, ship a change, and wait two weeks for the next cohort to tell them whether the guess was right. The feedback loop is measured in sprints.

OnboardLens shortens that loop to minutes by doing something analytics cannot: it sends an agent through the experience as a real persona and asks it to narrate, in first person, every point of friction — then turns that narration into a ranked list of fixes.

THE DIFFERENCE
Analytics tells you what happened — which step lost users. OnboardLens tells you why: the jargon, the missing “skip for now,” the form that asked too much too soon. It is qualitative usability insight at the speed and cost of an automated tool.

Three lenses across the funnel

Friction is not one problem — it shows up at different stages, and each needs a different lens. OnboardLens gives you three, and you pick one when you create a journey.

PLAN · AI SIMULATION

Pressure-test the onboarding you are designing

Describe your onboarding steps and ICP personas. Claude reviews each step as each persona, scoring clarity and friction and suggesting concrete fixes — before you have built anything. It is the fastest expert read on a flow that still lives in a Figma file or a doc, and it costs about a minute.

ACQUIRE · WEBSITE WALKTHROUGH

Score conversion friction on your live site

Claude visits your public website as a prospective visitor with a goal — say, deciding whether to book a demo — and scores conversion friction across four areas: messaging clarity, navigation and findability, conversion path, and trust. Let it free-browse toward the goal, or give it a defined page list to follow. No login required.

ACTIVATE · LIVE WALKTHROUGH

Onboard to your real product as a persona

Claude opens a real cloud browser, logs into your product, and works through onboarding as your ICP persona — clicking, typing, and narrating friction with live screenshots. It handles password, magic-link, injected-session, and human-handoff login, so even SSO- and MFA-gated products can be tested end to end.

Why “why” beats “what”

Because Claude narrates its reasoning as it goes, you do not just learn that a step failed — you learn the cause. The setup screen listed six integrations with no way to skip, so the persona felt trapped. The pricing page used a term only an engineer would recognize, so a finance buyer bounced. The signup form asked for a credit card before showing any value, so trust never formed. These are the specific, fixable causes that a drop-off chart can only hint at.

That qualitative read is what usability testing has always delivered — except a human study takes weeks to recruit, run, and synthesize. OnboardLens delivers the same kind of insight in the time it takes to make coffee, as many times as you want, across as many personas as you have.

From insight to a report your whole team can act on

A score on a screen does not change anything. The point of OnboardLens is the artifact at the end of every run:

  • A narrated walkthrough — every click and hesitation, in first person as the persona, with screenshots of exactly what Claude saw.
  • Friction and focus scores — an overall friction score, plus a per-area breakdown (messaging, navigation, conversion, trust) for website runs.
  • A prioritized action plan — ranked, severity-tagged recommendations, each with the specific fix and the moment in the walkthrough that revealed it. Built to hand straight to a PM or designer.
  • A shareable report and PDF — one click turns any run into a read-only link anyone can open with no login, or a branded PDF for a ticket, deck, or exec email.

That last point is the difference between a tool one person uses and a tool a team acts on. The friction is found, explained, ranked, and distributed — with no copy-paste in between.

Frequently asked questions

How do I test my SaaS onboarding for friction?

The most direct way is to have a real user go through your onboarding while narrating where they get confused or stuck. OnboardLens automates this: it opens a real browser and has Claude go through your product onboarding as your ICP persona — clicking, typing, and narrating friction in first person. It scores each point of friction and produces a prioritized action plan with the specific fix and the moment that revealed it, which you can share as a link or PDF.

Can AI evaluate my marketing website for conversion friction?

Yes. OnboardLens’s Website Walkthrough sends Claude through your public site as a prospective visitor with a goal — for example, deciding whether to book a demo. It scores conversion friction across messaging clarity, navigation and findability, conversion path, and trust and credibility, then recommends concrete fixes ranked by impact. No login is required because the site is public.

How is AI onboarding analysis different from analytics tools like Mixpanel?

Analytics tools tell you what happened — which step users dropped off at — but not why. OnboardLens tells you why. Because Claude walks the flow as a persona and narrates its reasoning, you learn that a step failed because the copy used jargon, or because there was no “skip for now” option, or because a form asked for too much too soon. It is qualitative usability insight at the speed and cost of an automated tool.

Can OnboardLens log into a product that requires authentication?

Yes. OnboardLens supports several login methods for live product walkthroughs: a test email and password, a magic-link flow using a disposable test inbox, an injected session, or a human handoff where you log in yourself in a live in-browser session — handling SSO, MFA, or captcha — and then hand control to Claude to continue from inside the product.

What do I get at the end of an OnboardLens run?

Every run produces a narrated walkthrough with screenshots, an overall friction score (plus per-area scores for websites), and a prioritized action plan of severity-tagged recommendations — each with the specific fix and the evidence from the walkthrough. The full report can be shared as a read-only link anyone can open without logging in, or downloaded as a branded PDF.
